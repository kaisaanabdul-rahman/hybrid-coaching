FAQ

Before you enroll

What happens after I pay?

Stripe confirms payment. You get an onboarding email for training history, schedule, and goal. Programming starts within 48 hours.

Which program should I pick?

New to lifting or coming back: start with 8 weeks. Already training and want weekly feedback: take 12 weeks. You want live sessions and a reserved spot: apply for 1-on-1.

Do I have to join Hybrid Arena?

No. Hybrid Coaching is standalone. Hybrid Arena is the community Kaisaan built — optional if you later want peers and culture around the work.

Is this in-person or online?

8-week and 12-week run online. 1-on-1 can be online or in person in Raleigh, depending on availability.

What if I only have a home gym?

Plans are written around the equipment you actually have.

Can I use Apple Pay or a credit card?

Yes. Stripe Checkout supports cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Link.

Refunds / pauses

Message within 7 days of purchase if something’s off and we’ll make it right case by case. Pausing mid-block is handled individually on the 12-week and 1-on-1 programs.
